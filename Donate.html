Why your support matters

Build systems that connect people to opportunity.

Mugaward Way Up Foundation is building a practical food ecosystem where rural producers and urban consumers can participate in a more connected local market.

Support helps strengthen the systems around that connection: production, market access, consumer organization, skills, logistics and responsible finance.

  • ✓
    Stronger opportunities for smallholder farmers.
  • ✓
    Better connections between producers and consumers.
  • ✓
    Practical skills and enterprise opportunities.
  • ✓
    Inclusive approaches to finance and participation.
Your support can strengthen

More than one part of the community.

Our model is designed so that different forms of support can reinforce different parts of the local livelihood ecosystem.

Quality production

Support practical approaches that help smallholder farmers improve production, quality, handling and market readiness.

🤝

Our approach

One connected model. Multiple points of impact.

We do not see rural farmers and urban consumers as separate audiences. We see them as partners in one livelihood ecosystem.

01

Strengthen production

Support farmers with practical production planning, quality improvement and market readiness.

02

Connect demand

Build stronger pathways between producers and organized urban consumers.

03

Support participation

Explore responsible finance and systems that allow both sides of the market to participate consistently.

04

Grow through partnerships

Work with communities and partners to test, learn and strengthen the model over time.
